01

Executive Summary & Strategic Positioning

Inside large enterprises, AI initiatives do not compete in isolation—they compete directly against capital equipment, commercial campaigns, and operational investments for the same discretionary growth budget. Yet bottom-up proposals arrive at central evaluation boards with uneven rigor, unsubstantiated efficiency claims, missing run-rate costs, and hidden cross-department duplication.

Case Studio is an agentic intake, enrichment, and portfolio-triage platform designed to bridge the gap between decentralized business teams and central investment governance committees. Rather than acting as a passive intake form, Case Studio embeds an interactive AI qualification coach at the front door of the investment funnel.

Architectural Decoupling: Case Studio separates its domain-agnostic qualification engine (static-RAG corporate grounding, schema-driven gap detection, semantic deduplication, cost-of-delay ranking, and strict fact-vs.-assumption provenance) from pluggable domain rubrics—configured via the sponsor’s Gate-1 Excel template and dynamically activating AI-specific data, architecture, and compliance probes when a proposal involves machine learning or agentic systems.

Case Submitter Business Team & Sponsor

Replaces static questionnaires with guided, context-aware business-case co-creation.

  • Silently grounds rough pitches against company strategy, org units, and profit pools via static RAG.
  • Asks 2–3 high-leverage follow-up questions per turn to enforce SMART KPIs and full lifecycle costs.
  • Separates Hard Savings from Soft Reinvested Capacity and captures Cost of Delay sensitivity.
  • Displays live provenance badges (Fact, Estimate, Inferred, Missing).

Case Manager Central Portfolio / AI Team

Eliminates manual backlog triage, duplicate PoC funding, and FIFO queue bottlenecks.

  • Distinguishes True Duplicates from Shared Capabilities / Overlapping Benefits across silos.
  • Prioritizes Gate-1 review queues using Weighted Shortest Job First (Cost of Delay ÷ Effort).
  • Flags stale assumptions (>30 days) when corporate RAG baselines change.
  • Exports harmonized, human-verified dossiers directly into the sponsor’s .xlsx workbook.

Empirical industry benchmarks highlight a severe conversion gap between bottom-up GenAI ideation and realized P&L impact. Four Failure Modes of Manual Gate-1 Evaluation

1. Central Triage Effort Bottleneck

Reviewing uneven submissions, chasing sponsors for baseline KPIs, and checking cross-unit overlaps at 3.5 hours/proposal across 900 cases consumes 3,150 hours (~1.75 FTE-years)—with 85%–90% (2,680–2,835 hrs) spent screening out redundant or low-ROI ideas before a single project reaches production.

2. Queue Latency & Cost of Delay (CoD)

Proposals spend 80%–90% of pre-approval time sitting idle in queues (6–12 weeks to 3–6 months). Across 100 top initiatives (conservatively valued at $60k/month each), a 3-month Gate-1 delay defers $18.0M in value—and permanently destroys $2.7M–$3.6M where 15%–20% of windows are time-perishable.

3. Assumption & Context Decay

When proposals sit unreviewed for months, baseline volumes, vendor capabilities, and internal IT architectures shift. Opening stale dossiers triggers a 30%–50% “re-qualification tax” on both reviewers and business teams just to re-validate outdated numbers.

4. Sponsor & Champion Attrition

Large enterprises experience 12%–18% annual internal mobility (~1.0%–1.5%/month). A 4-to-6-month Gate-1 backlog leaves 5%–9% of proposals without their original business sponsor or domain lead, turning viable ideas into orphaned backlog entries.

Why Case Studio Matters: J&J resolved its central bottleneck by training 47,000+ employees and embedding 6,000 internal forward-deployed “AI Specialists” into business functions. Most enterprises cannot afford thousands of embedded specialists. Case Studio automates that front-end qualification role at Gate 1—compressing cycle time from months to under 48 hours while enforcing CFO-grade rigor.

03

System Architecture & Agentic Workflow

Aligned with the ETH Agentic Systems Lab curriculum (Context Engineering & RAG, Structured Outputs, Human-in-the-Loop Governance, and Evals), Case Studio orchestrates a deterministic multi-stage state machine wrapped in a lightweight dual-view web interface.

Case Submitter replaces static intake forms with a targeted conversational loop that minimizes business-team friction while enforcing financial discipline:

  • Silent Corporate RAG Grounding: Before asking a single question, the agent queries static corporate documents (org structure, gross profit pools such as StandaPump 55% / Services 30% / CustoPump 15%, strategic priorities, and IT catalogs) to pre-fill domain alignment with exact document citations.
  • SMART KPI & Benefit Realization Coaching: Rejects vague claims (e.g., “improves technician efficiency”) and prompts the sponsor to quantify SMART metrics while distinguishing Soft Savings (reinvested FTE capacity with a phased ramp-up curve) from Hard Savings (headcount attrition/hiring cuts or direct OPEX reduction).
  • Lifecycle Cost Completeness: Verifies separation between one-off build phases (MVP, Pilot, Productization) and recurring Operations & Token run-rate expenses—flagging omitted run-rate estimates explicitly as Agent Inferred or Missing.
  • Window Sensitivity & Cost of Delay Probe: Captures monthly run-rate value and classifies opportunities as Linear Value vs. Time-Perishable Value (with an explicit value-expiration date) so urgent cases never languish in FIFO queues.
  • Pluggable AI Domain Extension: Dynamically activates data-readiness, shared-capability, and compliance/risk probes whenever a submission involves AI/ML or agentic workflows.

Case Manager gives the central AI / portfolio team a harmonized, deduplicated view across all incoming proposals while preserving human decision authority:

  • Two-Stage Semantic Deduplication: First retrieves candidate neighbors using hybrid dense + keyword embeddings across problem statement, affected workflow, and expected benefit; then runs a structured LLM reasoning pass classifying pairs as True Duplicate, Shared Capability / Overlapping Benefit (e.g., Sales and Service independently pitching assistants over the same product manuals), or Distinct—preserving links to original submitters and sponsors.
  • Cost-of-Delay (WSJF) Queue Ranking: Ranks Gate-1 review order by Monthly Cost of Delay ÷ Estimated Effort rather than submission timestamp, ensuring time-sensitive opportunities are triaged first.
  • Staleness & RAG-Drift Detection: Stamps every field with a verified_at timestamp and flags proposals sitting unreviewed past 30 days or impacted by updates to corporate strategy/IT RAG documents.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Governance & Excel Bridge: Reviewers confirm, split, or merge proposed clusters, inspect unresolved questions side-by-side, and export the standardized portfolio directly into the sponsor’s .xlsx Gate-1 workbook via openpyxl.

Every Gate-1 field is typed as a structured QualifiedField object so LLM inferences and submitter estimates can never masquerade as verified facts during Excel export:

models/case_record.py 
from datetime import datetime
from typing import Literal, Optional, List
from pydantic import BaseModel, Field

ProvenanceStatus = Literal[
    "verified_fact",      # Backed by uploaded doc or static company RAG
    "submitter_estimate", # Stated by business team without hard evidence
    "agent_inferred",     # Extrapolated by agent (e.g., missing Ops run-rate)
    "missing"             # Unresolved gap flagged for Gate-1 review
]

class QualifiedField(BaseModel):
    value: Optional[str] = None
    status: ProvenanceStatus = "missing"
    source_citation: Optional[str] = None
    open_question: Optional[str] = None
    verified_at: Optional[datetime] = None

class LifecycleFinancials(BaseModel):
    build_and_pilot_cost: QualifiedField
    productization_cost: QualifiedField
    annual_ops_run_rate: QualifiedField
    hard_savings_eur: QualifiedField       # Headcount / direct OPEX reduction
    soft_savings_fte: QualifiedField       # Reinvested capacity + ramp-up curve
    monthly_cost_of_delay: QualifiedField
    window_sensitivity: Literal["linear", "time_perishable", "unknown"] = "unknown"
    value_expiration_date: Optional[str] = None
    smart_kpis: List[QualifiedField]

class AIDomainExtension(BaseModel):
    # Activated dynamically when proposal involves AI/ML/Agentic systems
    data_sources_and_readiness: QualifiedField
    shared_capability_tags: List[str]
    key_risks_and_dependencies: QualifiedField

class CaseRecord(BaseModel):
    case_id: str
    title: str
    domain_unit: QualifiedField
    sponsor_and_team: QualifiedField
    strategic_objective: QualifiedField
    problem_and_workflow: QualifiedField
    proposed_solution: QualifiedField
    financials: LifecycleFinancials
    ai_extension: Optional[AIDomainExtension] = None
    cluster_relation: Literal["distinct", "shared_dependency", "duplicate"] = "distinct"
    related_case_ids: List[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    cluster_rationale: Optional[str] = None
